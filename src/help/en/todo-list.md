# To-Do List

## Purpose
To-Do List lets you create, manage, and track tasks. Your tasks are saved automatically and will still be there the next time you open the app.

## How to add a task
1. Type your task in the input field at the top.
2. Press **Enter** or click **Add**.
3. The task will appear in the list below.

## Managing tasks

- **Mark as done**: click the checkbox on the left side of a task. A strikethrough will appear to indicate it is completed.
- **Mark as pending again**: click the checkbox again to uncheck it.
- **Edit**: hover over a task and click the pencil icon. Press **Enter** to save or **Escape** to cancel.
- **Delete**: hover over a task and click the trash icon to remove it permanently.

## Clearing completed tasks
Once you have one or more completed tasks, a **Clear N completed** button will appear at the bottom. Click it to remove all completed tasks at once.

## Progress bar
The progress bar shows how many of your tasks have been completed out of the total.

## Notes
- Tasks are listed with the most recently added at the top.
- Deleting a task is permanent and cannot be undone.
